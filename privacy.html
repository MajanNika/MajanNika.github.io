Privacy Policy - My AI-office

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Who uses this app

My AI-office is a private application used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app accesses the owner's Google Calendar, Google Sheets, Google Drive, Google Docs, Gmail and Apps Script data through Google APIs.

How the data is used

The data is used only to perform tasks the owner requests, such as creating calendar events or working with documents. The data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where the data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's private server.

How to revoke access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the owner's GitHub profile: github.com/MajanNika.
